# Manchester City 2025/26 Performance Analysis
A Power BI project analyzing Manchester City's performance throughout the 2025/26 season across the Premier League, UEFA Champions League, FA Cup, and EFL Cup.


## Project Overview
This project was built as an end-to-end data analysis project to practice the complete analytical process — from collecting and preparing raw football data to data modeling, analysis, DAX development, and building an interactive Power BI report.

The final report provides a structured view of Manchester City's season, moving from the overall season outcome to team-level performance and individual squad contributions.


## Objectives
- Evaluate Manchester City's overall performance during the 2025/26 season.
- Compare performance across the four competitions.
- Analyze how team performance developed throughout the season.
- Examine home and away performance.
- Evaluate attacking and defensive performance.
- Analyze tactical formations and their results.
- Explore player contributions, playing time, attacking output, defensive actions, and discipline.
- Build a professional Power BI project using a structured analytical data model.


## Data Sources
The project primarily uses data collected from **FBref**, supplemented by official competition sources where additional data was required:
- **FBref** — Primary data source for the project, covering match-level and player-level statistics across all four competitions.
- **PremierLeague.com** — Premier League standings and matchweek position data, as well as player tackle statistics used in the defensive analysis.
- **UEFA.com** — UEFA Champions League tackle data, including Tackles, Tackles Won, and Tackles Lost, used to supplement the defensive analysis.


## Data Preparation & Modeling
Raw data from different competitions and statistical categories was prepared using **Excel and Power Query**.
The preparation process included cleaning, standardizing, merging, and appending multiple source tables before creating the final analytical datasets.
The Power BI model was structured using a **Star Schema approach**, separating fact tables from supporting dimensions.
Main analytical tables:
- `Fact_Matches`
- `Fact_Player_Stats`
- `Fact_Player_Tackles`
- `Fact_League_Weeks`
- `Dim_Players`

Additional dimension and helper tables were created inside Power BI to support date filtering, dynamic metrics, and report interactions.


## Report Pages

### 1. Overview
Provides a concise summary of Manchester City's season across the four competitions, showing the final outcome of each competition and the team's progression through the Premier League season.

### 2. Team Analysis
Analyzes Manchester City as a team through overall KPIs, performance trends, home vs away performance, attacking and defensive balance, and tactical formations.
The page can be filtered by competition and season month, allowing the analysis to adapt to different periods of the season.

### 3. Squad Analysis
Examines how individual players contributed to the team's performance through three analytical sections:
- **Playing Time** — minutes, appearances, starts, substitutions, and squad usage.
- **Attacking** — goals, assists, goal contributions, shooting volume, shooting accuracy, and conversion.
- **Defensive & Discipline** — interceptions, tackles, fouls, yellow cards, and red cards.


## Key Insights
- Manchester City finished the Premier League season as **runner-up with 78 points**, seven points behind Arsenal.
- Across the full analyzed season, City played **60 matches**, recording **40 wins, 10 draws, and 10 losses**, for a **66.7% win rate**.
- The team scored **129 goals** and conceded **54**, highlighting a strong positive attacking-defensive balance across competitions.
- City averaged approximately **61% possession**, reinforcing the team's possession-oriented style.
- Home and away possession was very similar — approximately **62.5% at home** and **60.3% away** — indicating that the team's approach remained relatively consistent regardless of venue.
- The **4-2-3-1 formation** produced the highest win rate among the frequently used formations shown in the report, at **84.6% across 13 matches**.
- **Erling Haaland** was the team's leading scorer with **38 goals** and also recorded the highest goal-contribution rate shown in the report at **1.02 per 90 minutes**.
- **Rayan Cherki** led the squad in assists with **15**, while also ranking among the team's leading attacking contributors.
- Haaland also generated by far the highest shooting volume with **171 shots**, while converting **22.2%** of his attempts.
- Playing time was concentrated among several core players, with Haaland, Nico O'Reilly, Matheus Nunes, Gianluigi Donnarumma, and Bernardo Silva all recording more than 3,800 minutes.
- **Abdukodir Khusanov** recorded the most interceptions in the squad analysis with **54**.
- Within the Premier League and Champions League tackle dataset, **Matheus Nunes** won **44 of 62 tackles (71.0%)**, while Khusanov recorded the highest displayed tackle success rate among the leading players at **74.3%**.
